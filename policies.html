Policies

Data and privacy

Creature Seekers does not use any analytics tools or cookies to monitor users. Additionally, no personal data is collected, stored or processed by Creature Seekers.

Some pages contain embedded surveys hosted by a third-party service (Jotform), which may set cookies and process data necessary to provide the service, according to their own privacy policy (opens in a new tab). These surveys are optional, anonymous and do not collect personally identifiable data.

Accessibility

This site was designed with accessibility in mind, including responsive design, keyboard navigation, colour choices and more. If you have accessibility needs which are not being met, please use the Issue Report form.
