How does Creature Seekers work?

Step 1

Around St Andrew's Park this summer are six stations featuring insects which can be found locally, in different habitat areas.

You can follow the trail in the "Map" page, or explore in your way!

Step 2

At each station you'll find artwork and a codeword related to the insect you've found.

Step 3

Go to the "Codeword" page and type in the unique codeword for that insect.

Step 4

You'll unlock full-colour art of that insect, plus a factfile about their life-cycle and how you can help them out in your area

Step 5

Complete the trail and unlock a quiz about the insects featured in the trail!

Step 6

Good luck, have fun, enjoy creature seeking!

Overview

This site was made as part of the Avon Wildlife Trust's Wildlife Champion scheme.

Data and privacy

This site collects a limited amount of data about how the site is used. This will be used solely for quality assurance and monitoring usage to gauge uptake and effectiveness. We do not collect any personal data, and we will not pass any individuals' data onto third parties.
