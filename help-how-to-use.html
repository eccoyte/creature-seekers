How does Creature Seekers work?

Let me take you through it, step by step!

Step 1

Around St Andrews Park this summer are six stations (wooden signs) featuring insects which can be found locally, in different habitat areas.

You can follow the trail on the map page, or explore in your own way.

Step 2

At each station you'll find artwork and a unique codeword related to the insect you've found.

Step 3

Go to the codeword page and type in the codeword you've found!

Step 4

You'll unlock full-colour art of that insect, plus factfiles about their lifecycle and how you can help them out in your area. These can be found on the profiles page.

Step 5

Complete the trail and unlock a quiz about the insects featured in the trail! All the information you need to get top score will be in the profiles. Find this on the quiz page.

Step 6

Good luck, have fun, and enjoy creature seeking!
